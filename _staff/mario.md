## Professional career

Mario received the bachelor’s degree in Industrial Engineering from the Technological University of Pereira (Colombia) in 2015, a master’s degree in Industrial Engineering from the University of the North (Colombia) in 2020, and a master’s degree in Digital Business in 2024. He has worked in secondary education, university education, and industries related to electrical energy. He holds a PhD in Industrial Engineering from the University of Zaragoza (Spain), awarded in 2025, within the Advanced Information Systems Laboratory (IAAA) of the Aragon Institute of Engineering Research (I3A). Currently, he is pursuing a master’s degree in Applied Statistics at the University of Nariño (Colombia), with expected completion in 2027, and works as an Assistant Professor (Profesor Ayudante Doctor) at the University of Zaragoza. His research interests are Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Data Science in general. His master’s and doctoral studies were supported by scholarships granted by MINCIENCIAS.
