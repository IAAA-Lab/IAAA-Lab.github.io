## Professional career

Jorge Soria holds a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Engineering (with a specialization in Computing) from the University of Zaragoza, obtained in 2026. His Bachelor's Thesis, a collaborative project between the Advanced Information Systems Laboratory (IAAA) and the Catalysis and Reactor Engineering Group (CREG), focused on gas chromatograph calibration using machine learning techniques. The thesis was funded by the I3A scholarship program and selected for an oral presentation at the "XV Jornada de Jóvenes Investigadores e Investigadoras". He is currently working as a member of the IAAA research group.
